How scores, watch rankings, and filters work in Cairo time
🎛️ Multi-Filter Combinations Tap multiple filter buttons (e.g. 🔥 Best + ☀️ Evening) to combine them simultaneously. Tap All to clear active filters.
👁️ Hype Meter (0–100) An excitement rating balancing spread competitiveness and combined O/U scoring pace. Note: Highly lopsided matchups with heavy win-probability favorites may feature lower hype scores despite heavy model confidence.
📊 Model Pick (Pre-Game Projection) Calculated before kickoff/tipoff from ESPN projections and betting lines. On finished games, it shows the pre-game favorite alongside the final score.
☀️ Evening vs. 🦉 Late Night Evening games fall between 19:00 and 00:30 Cairo time. Games kicking off between midnight and 05:00 AM are tagged as Late Night.
